{%- comment -%} Builds a mailto: URL for claiming or sponsoring a festival page. Params: festival (page/document), action ("claim" | "sponsor") Spaces are encoded as %20 (url_encode emits "+", which mail apps show literally). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign fm = include.festival -%} {%- assign fm_url = site.url | append: fm.url -%} {%- if include.action == "sponsor" -%} {%- assign fm_subject = site.contact.sponsor_subject_prefix | append: fm.title -%} {%- capture fm_body -%}Hi Florida Food Festival team, I'm interested in sponsoring the {{ fm.title }} page: {{ fm_url }} Name: Company or brand: Website: Timing and budget: Thanks!{%- endcapture -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign fm_subject = site.contact.claim_subject_prefix | append: fm.title -%} {%- capture fm_body -%}Hi Florida Food Festival team, I'd like to claim the {{ fm.title }} page: {{ fm_url }} Name: Role with the festival: Official email or website we can use to verify you: Updates to make (dates, tickets, photos, links): Thanks!{%- endcapture -%} {%- endif -%} mailto:{{ site.contact.listings_email }}?subject={{ fm_subject | url_encode | replace: "+", "%20" }}&body={{ fm_body | url_encode | replace: "+", "%20" }}
