Organizers: claim your festival page

Claiming your page is a done-for-you service. Send us your dates, ticket links, photos and lineup, and our team updates the page and adds a Verified by organizer badge. Because every update is made by hand, claiming is a paid service, with pricing on request. It can be combined with a featured listing or sponsorship.

Our rules

  • Facts aren't for sale. Sponsors never control a festival's dates, location, prices or the accuracy of our listings, and no one can pay to change what we report about another event.
  • Paid is always labeled. Every paid placement carries a visible “Sponsored” label, and sponsor links use rel="sponsored".
  • You can measure it. Outbound festival links are tagged with utm_source=floridafoodfestival so organizers and sponsors can see the traffic we send.
  • Food-first fit. We accept sponsors that fit a family-friendly food and travel audience, and we may decline placements that don't.
