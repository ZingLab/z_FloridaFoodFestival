Cedar Key is one of the top producers of farm-raised clams in the nation, and its fall seafood festival puts them front and center. The island town fills with seafood vendors, artists and visitors for a weekend each October.

With one road onto the island, traffic and parking are tight. Come early or stay overnight to enjoy the sunset over the Gulf.
