Burgers & Brews started in 2016 as an Independence Day weekend party in downtown Vero Beach. It has since moved to the Indian River County Fairgrounds, where local restaurants compete for Best Burger in Indian River County.

Every ticket supports United Against Poverty's grocery, workforce and family programs.
