The Grant Seafood Festival has been a Space Coast staple since the 1960s, run by volunteers from the small community of Grant-Valkaria. The riverfront weekend serves classic Florida seafood and supports local scholarships and community projects.

It is a relaxed, old-Florida style festival, ideal for families and anyone who prefers a down-home atmosphere to big-city events.
