Destin calls itself the World's Luckiest Fishing Village, and its seafood festival celebrates that reputation each October. Local restaurants and vendors serve fresh Gulf catch with harbor views.

October brings cooler weather and thinner crowds than summer on the Emerald Coast, making it a great time to visit.
