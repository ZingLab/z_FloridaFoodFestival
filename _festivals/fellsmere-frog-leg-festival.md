The tiny town of Fellsmere in Indian River County hosts a festival devoted to fried frog legs, and it has become one of Florida's most famous quirky food events. Volunteers fry enormous quantities of frog legs, gator tail and catfish over a long weekend in January.

If you've never tried frog legs, this is the place: they're mild, tender and usually described as somewhere between chicken and fish.
