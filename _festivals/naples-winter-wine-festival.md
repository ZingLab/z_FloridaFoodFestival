The Naples Winter Wine Festival is one of the most celebrated charity wine events in the world. Each winter, renowned vintners and chefs gather for intimate dinners and a live auction that raises millions for Collier County children's programs.

Attendance is by package and limited, so it is a bucket-list event rather than a casual outing. Even if you are not attending, the festival's influence ripples through Naples' dining scene that week.
