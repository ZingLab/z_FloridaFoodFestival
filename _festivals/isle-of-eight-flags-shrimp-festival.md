Fernandina Beach is widely called the birthplace of the modern shrimping industry, and the Isle of Eight Flags Shrimp Festival has celebrated that heritage since the 1960s. Historic downtown fills with shrimp in every style, an art show and live music.

Admission is free, which helps make it one of Northeast Florida's biggest weekends. Park early or use the shuttle, and save room for multiple shrimp dishes.
