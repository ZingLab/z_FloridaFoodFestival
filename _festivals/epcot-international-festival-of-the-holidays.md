The Festival of the Holidays wraps EPCOT's festival year with a cozy, seasonal menu. Holiday Kitchens spread across World Showcase serve dishes tied to celebrations from Hanukkah to Las Posadas, alongside a cookie stroll that has become a fan favorite.

Evenings are the main event, with the Candlelight Processional drawing big crowds. The weeks between Christmas and New Year's are among the busiest of the year at Walt Disney World, so early December weekdays are the sweet spot for relaxed grazing.
