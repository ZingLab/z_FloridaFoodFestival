Sebastian ShrimpFest fills Riverview Park each spring with shrimp dishes, food trucks and friendly competition, from a best-chili contest to pie baking. It's organized by the Rotary Club of Sebastian with the City of Sebastian.

Admission and parking are free, making it an easy family outing on the lagoon.
