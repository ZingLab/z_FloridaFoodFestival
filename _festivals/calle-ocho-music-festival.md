Calle Ocho is Little Havana's grand street party, taking over SW 8th Street with music stages, dancing and a long corridor of Cuban and Latin American food. It is the closing event of Carnaval Miami and a celebration of the city's Latin heritage.

Crowds are enormous, so arrive early and plan your exit. The food is the reason many locals come: cafecito, croquetas, pastelitos and lechón from neighborhood favorites.
