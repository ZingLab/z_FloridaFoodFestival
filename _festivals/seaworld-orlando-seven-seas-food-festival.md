SeaWorld Orlando's Seven Seas Food Festival turns spring weekends into a park-wide tasting tour. Booths serve international small plates and plenty of craft beer, and sampler lanyards make it easy to try a lot for less.

Concert nights are the busiest. Annual pass holders often visit across several weekends to work through the menu.
