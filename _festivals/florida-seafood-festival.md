Billed as Florida's oldest maritime event, the Florida Seafood Festival celebrates Apalachicola's seafood heritage each November at Battery Park. Expect oyster shucking contests, a blue crab race, a parade and plenty of Gulf seafood.

Apalachicola is a small town, so lodging books up quickly for festival weekend. Consider staying in nearby Eastpoint, St. George Island or Port St. Joe.
