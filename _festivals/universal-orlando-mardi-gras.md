Universal Orlando's Mardi Gras pairs nighttime parades and concerts with food booths inspired by Carnaval celebrations around the world. New Orleans favorites share the menu with dishes from Brazil, the Caribbean and Latin America.

The event runs on select dates through early spring. Concert nights draw the biggest crowds, so check the calendar if you would rather graze in peace.
