Hernando County has a long blueberry-festival tradition. After the downtown Brooksville Blueberry Festival ended, Community Markets & Events launched the Hernando County Blueberry Festival at Florida Classic Park in 2026, with free admission and parking.

Expect blueberry desserts and drinks, local growers, vendors and live entertainment on a 50-acre site. Nearby U-pick farms make it easy to turn the day into a berry-picking trip.
