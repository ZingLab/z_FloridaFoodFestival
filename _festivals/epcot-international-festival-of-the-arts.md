EPCOT's Festival of the Arts brings together visual, culinary and performing arts. The Food Studios lean into presentation, plating small dishes like works on a canvas, and the festival tends to feel a little less hectic than the fall food and wine season.

Florida's mild winter weather makes this one of the most comfortable times to graze World Showcase. Concerts and workshops fill weekends, while weekdays leave room to linger over the art.
