Tampa's Cuban sandwich, with its signature layer of Genoa salami, reflects the city's Cuban, Spanish and Italian heritage. The International Cuban Sandwich Festival celebrates it with a competition among restaurants and plenty of tasting.

Pair a visit with a stroll through Ybor City's historic district and a café con leche.
