Since 1970, Everglades City has celebrated its fishing heritage with the Everglades Seafood Festival. The small town swells for a weekend of stone crab, gator tail, frog legs and live country music.

Pair the festival with an airboat ride or a paddle in the Ten Thousand Islands to make a full Everglades weekend.
