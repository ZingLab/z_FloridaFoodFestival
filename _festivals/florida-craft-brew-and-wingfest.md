Sunrise Rotary Vero Beach's Florida Craft Brew & Wingfest takes over Royal Palm Pointe each February. Restaurants battle for the best wings while guests sample hundreds of craft beers, with bands on multiple stages.

Admission is free; buy a tasting bracelet if you plan to sample beers.
