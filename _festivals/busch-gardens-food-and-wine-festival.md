Busch Gardens Tampa Bay's Food & Wine Festival pairs spring weekends with tasting booths and a lineup of headline concerts. It is one of the Tampa Bay area's favorite ways to combine roller coasters with a culinary tour.

Concert weekends sell the most tickets, so arrive early if you want shorter lines at the booths before the show.
