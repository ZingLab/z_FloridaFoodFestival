The EPCOT International Food & Wine Festival is the heavyweight of Florida food events and one of the longest food festivals in the country. Each fall, Walt Disney World turns World Showcase into a walkable tasting menu, with Global Marketplace booths serving small plates and drinks inspired by cuisines from around the globe.

Because the festival runs for roughly three months, it rewards planning. Weekday mornings are calmer, weekends and evenings fill quickly, and many guests track their tastings on the festival passport. Premium experiences such as wine dinners and mixology seminars sell out in advance, so book those as soon as they are announced.
