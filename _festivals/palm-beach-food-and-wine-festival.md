The Palm Beach Food & Wine Festival gathers celebrity and local chefs for a long weekend of tastings, dinners and demonstrations across the Palm Beaches. Its December timing makes it a highlight of the early high season.

Events are ticketed individually; the Grand Tasting is the best introduction if you only choose one.
