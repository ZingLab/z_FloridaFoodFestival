The Sebastian Clambake celebrates life on the Indian River Lagoon with a weekend of clams prepared nearly every way. Volunteers from the community staff the entire festival, and proceeds from the Sebastian Clambake Foundation go to local nonprofit capital projects.

Admission is free, so locals tend to drop in more than once. Come for steamed clams and chowder, stay for live bands along the river.
