Swamp cabbage, the tender heart of the sabal palm, was a staple for Florida pioneers. LaBelle's Swamp Cabbage Festival has honored that tradition since the 1960s with a parade, rodeo and plenty of swamp cabbage dishes.

It's an authentic slice of rural Florida culture along the Caloosahatchee River.
