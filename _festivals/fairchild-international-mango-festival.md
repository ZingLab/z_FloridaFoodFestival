Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden is a world leader in mango research, and its International Mango Festival is a summer pilgrimage for fruit lovers. Visitors taste rare cultivars, learn from experts and take home trees of their own.

July in Miami is hot, so arrive when the gates open and stay hydrated as you explore the garden.
